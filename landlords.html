For Landlords

RateMyCrib is a platform for honest student reviews of off-campus housing at UIUC. We understand that landlords may have questions or concerns about how the platform works and how reviews affect your business. This page explains our approach.

Good landlords benefit from RateMyCrib. If you provide quality housing, responsive maintenance, and fair lease terms, your reviews will reflect that and students will choose you over the competition.

Our Standards

All reviews on RateMyCrib must reflect genuine tenant experiences. We do not allow fabricated reviews, spam, defamation, or threats. Our Terms of Service require that every review be truthful and based on a real rental experience. Anyone can report a review with the Report button on it, and we look at every report.

Disputing a Review

If you believe a review contains factually inaccurate statements that are defamatory, you may submit a detailed dispute to ratemycrib@proton.me. Please include the specific review, the statements you believe are inaccurate, and any supporting evidence.

We take disputes seriously and will review them on a case-by-case basis. We aim to respond within 14 business days. We don't remove a review just because it's negative or you disagree with it: honest opinions about a real experience, like "maintenance was slow", generally stay up. We remove reviews that are fake, threatening, expose someone's personal information, or state false facts, and we don't rewrite reviews.

No one can pay us to remove, edit or hide a review, change a rating, or change the order reviews appear in. Paying and non-paying landlords are treated the same. If we ever offer paid placements or reports, anything paid on the site will be labeled "Sponsored" and won't change any rating.

What You Can Do

The best way to improve your rating on RateMyCrib is to improve your service. Respond to maintenance requests promptly. Communicate clearly with tenants. Return security deposits fairly and on time. Students notice and they write about it.

You can also invite your tenants to leave honest reviews: more reviews give a more accurate picture of your business. Invite all of them, not just the happy ones, and don't offer anything in return or ask for a positive review. Reviews that were paid for, rewarded or written by staff break our Terms and are removed.

Legal Framework

Reviews are written by tenants, and Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act generally protects sites that host what their users write. We don't write or edit reviews, and they represent the opinions of the people who wrote them. "Verified Illini" means the reviewer proved they have an @illinois.edu email address, not that we checked their account of events. Ratings and tags on landlord pages are calculated from the reviews. For full details, see our Terms of Service.

Contact

For disputes, questions, or general inquiries, email us at ratemycrib@proton.me. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
