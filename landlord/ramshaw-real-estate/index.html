3.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Would not rent again · $550/mo · 1110 w stoughton · Lease: Fall 2024 – Spring 2026

The apartments themselves aren’t the nicest but they are quite cheap, so you get what you pay for. Still clearly all the appliances are the cheapest and often have issues. Ramshaw maintenance is just awful, if you leave a request on the portal there is a 90% chance it will never be looked at. If you try and call maintenance directly they will most likely not even pick up and it will go straight to voicemail. The building has had consistent issues the entire time I’ve lived here, for example the doors to the building. They installed new ones with actual locks which was nice, but unfortunately the doors fall apart every week so they can’t even install a door correctly. There was a broken window that was haphazardly covered up and it took months to fix. The washer and dryers for the building never ever worked and would steal change all the time, though they are apparently replacing them so we will see how well that works out, but with their current track record I don’t have my hopes super high.

2.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Verified Illini · Would not rent again · $750/mo · 1901 N Lincoln Ave (Capstone Quarters) · Lease: Fall 2022 – Summer 2024

Because these apartments are leased individually, my after my roommate moved out, Ramshaw was running 1-2 tours a day for 2 weeks in my apartment. Additionally, they don’t post utilities on time in your portal, then slap you with late fees when you don’t pay them with rent (but they won’t be posted for 2 weeks so you can’t). Don’t even try to get your deposit back, they will make up damages that don’t exist and keep it all, and then try to charge you hundreds more on top of that. Walking home on N lincoln is not safe at night, so you need to take the 22. Good luck if you need to be on time, though.
