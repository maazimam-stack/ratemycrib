4.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Verified Illini · Would rent again · $845/mo · 901 Western Avenue · Lease: Fall 2025 – Summer 2026

This apartment is located very close to ISR for reference. The location is great if you want a quiet space and most of your classes are either on the main quad or north of green. It is in a fairly quiet area and as such is nice for those who don't want too much noise. The building is secure, requiring a key fob to enter the building in addition to your unit's actual key. They have an indoor parking garage as well. It includes a small gym, but I don't usually see people inside. Rent includes everything BUT electricity cost. Electricity cost is split evenly between all tenants of the unit as an additional charge each month. This can vary month by month depending on how much electricity you use, but I found the charge to be a reasonable amount, even when my roommates and I are all homebodies and spent most of our time on our computers which I imagine drives up electricity usage. When we first moved in, the place was very dusty, there were a couple chairs that had seen better days, and the AC was not working. We cleaned up the dust, and called maintenance about the chairs and the AC. Those were quickly resolved once we contacted them about it, and basically all subsequent maintenance requests have had no issues and were done in a timely manner. The internet connection here is also very good at 1000 mbps. The wifi router is eh, but the Ethernet connections allow you to fully utilize those speeds. Includes an in-unit washer and dryer. No issues with pests (I was on the third floor if that matters) Now, the complaints: Besides the upstairs neighbor being loud, I actually have very few issues with this apartment. The only real one would be the trash and recycling. The building's management has issues cleaning out the trash/recycling room regularly at times, and so there have been several instances where the bins have become full or overflowing, and you cannot throw out your trash. Once, it was so bad that the door to the trash/recycling room was actually blocked by trash bags and I could not get through the door to throw garbage out. Overall, I find that this is a great place to live. Appliances in good working order and maintenance is responsive. Quiet location, good internet service. Most utilities are covered by the monthly rent and are set up for you, which is convenient. The only reason I wouldn't rate this 5 stars is because of the garbage problem, but otherwise I would recommend living here.

+ Great Location · + Fast Repairs · + Quiet · + Furnished
