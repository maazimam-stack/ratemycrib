4.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Verified Illini · Would rent again · $1049/mo · 707 S 4th St · Lease: Fall 2025 – Summer 2026

The location on 4th and Green is great. Can get pretty much everywhere I need to go as a student in the college of LAS. Never have to take the bus. No security deposit, excellent amenities, and really fast and responsive maintenance in my experience. Our washing machine and dryer both broke and maintenance replaced them both pretty quickly. Appliances are not the best, the dishwasher does not work well at all. However, the walls can be quite thin, you will absolutely hear your neighbors if they are loud. The leasing office feels the need to add random charges before move in with no explanation, but I asked for them to drop it and they did. They could do better with communication. The random inspections with short notice have caught me off guard, and they raised the rent this year. It can be annoying at times dealing with some of the fixtures and leasing office, but the good outweighs the bad here in my honest opinion. I would do it again, but you could probably find better if you tried. Spots filled up quick for next year, so its coveted. I would tour and sign early if you are interested.
