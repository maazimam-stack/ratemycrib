4.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Verified Illini · Would rent again · $775/mo · 502 E John St · Lease: Fall 2022 – Spring 2026

Maintenance speed is pretty good, especially for urgent requests. Rent has increased every year for 3 yrs straight. Jsm is pretty good about addressing concerns and they have a lot of events and stuff for residents, plus the gym and study center very nearby which has been helpful. The apartment is in a relatively safe area but there has been like shots fired nearby a couple of times, probably just bc it’s near bars. It’s hella loud like nonstop, fat sandwich stays up and plays music til 3am basically every night. Amazing apartment tho, great location, spacious. The applicances and such have been decent but some have broken, but JSM is mostly decent about fixing, a bit hesitant to replace but we’ve had our microwave and fridge/freezer replaced after complaining a bunch. Overall would recommend despite the caveats.
