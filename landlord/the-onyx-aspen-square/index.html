1.0 / 5 · Jul 2026 · Would not rent again · $675/mo · 1321 N. Lincoln Ave. · Lease: Fall 2024 – Summer 2026

Only lease here if you don’t value any of your belongings. Paper thin walls, persistent bugs, horrendous communication from truly incompetent office staff. Left for a trip over winter break and received a voicemail at 8:30 PM the next day: “Call us in the morning for an update about your unit.” Screamed bad news so I called back, got told the upstairs neighbors left their window open which cracked a pipe and flooded my apartment but they danced around the severity of the damage. Only learned over the next several days that an inch of water had covered our whole unit. We got a whopping $200 off one month’s rent! But how do you try to avoid telling someone all their stuff got soaked in your awful build?? Finally leaving and move-out is next to impossible with multiple construction projects blocking all entrances and exits. Apparently I’m not the first one to call the city about it, fingers crossed something actually happens but I won’t hold my breath. The only positive of this whole experience has been the maintenance team. They are kind, can actually communicate, and know that they are working in a shithole.

4.0 / 5 · Mar 2026 · Verified Illini · Would rent again · $810/mo · 1321 N Lincoln Ave (ONYX) · Lease: Summer 2025 – Summer 2026

Utilities included except power. Fully furnished. Maintenance is quick and leasing office is responsive and friendly. The distance is a little farther from campus, but is directly on the 22 Illini bus line. Free parking. Maintenance does need to enter sometimes (especially during freeze warnings) but they send out notices. During freeze warnings, you must keep your water dripping and the heat above 70 (they check). I’d say the rent is fair.
