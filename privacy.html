Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version. Reviews are anonymous: they're never stored with an email address, an account or anything that identifies your device. Giving us an email is always optional; it goes on a separate list, every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and we never sell it or share it with landlords. Leases you scan never leave your browser.

The rest of this page is the detail. It applies to ratemycrib.net, including the review form, the landlord pages and the lease scanner. "We" means RateMyCrib, a student-run project in Champaign-Urbana. Questions: ratemycrib@proton.me.

1. What we collect

When you post a review

What you put in the form: the landlord, your star rating, whether you'd rent from them again, your review, when your lease started (and ended, if you say), and anything optional you add: the street address (we remove unit numbers before saving it), neighborhood, monthly rent, whether you got your security deposit back, area scores and tags. We also store the hour it was posted (not the minute); the site shows only the date.

A review is never stored with an email address, a sign-in account, an IP address or a device identifier. Reviews posted before October 30, 2026 also stored a "device fingerprint" (a code made from browser settings, used to catch duplicates). We no longer collect it, and it has never been shown on the site.

If you add a landlord that isn't listed yet, we save its name so others can pick it. Student-added landlords get a page on Google only after we've checked them.

If you give us an email address (optional)

There are five places you can do this:

  • Verification in the review form: we email a one-time code to your @illinois.edu address through our database provider, Supabase. If you enter it, your review gets the "Verified Illini" badge, and the temporary sign-in account is deleted when the review posts (or within a couple of hours if you don't post). Verifying adds your address to our list for occasional updates, as the form says; "Undo" right after verifying takes it off again.
  • "Email for updates" in the review form.
  • "Unlock every review" on landlord pages and Browse by Street.
  • "Lease-season tips" at the bottom of each page.
  • Lease scanner early access (the "Smarter Lease Scanner" box).

For each address we keep: the address, which box it came from, the exact wording that box showed you (as a record of what you agreed to), the day we first got it, whether you've unsubscribed, and a random code for your unsubscribe link. That list holds no reviews, landlords or times, and it's kept apart from reviews. Your email is never shown on the site.

When you use the site

To see what's working, we record pages viewed and features used: for example searches (the words you typed), the rent and landlord you enter in "Am I Overpaying?", the major and year you pick in "Near your classes", that a lease scan ran and how many clauses it flagged (never the lease itself), and shares. With each one we record:

  • a random visitor ID that resets every calendar month, so we can count people rather than clicks. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we don't create one;
  • a random ID for the visit, which ends when you close the tab;
  • the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), screen size and the page;
  • the website that sent you (just the site, like reddit.com, never the page) and any campaign code in the link (like ?ref=flyer).

A few are recorded more coarsely: anything to do with the review form (the buttons that open it, closing it without posting, posting, and the thank-you screen after posting), verifying, and giving us an email address. These carry no visitor or visit ID, no screen size and only the hour. Databases also keep rows in the order they were saved, so every 15 minutes ours saves the recent ones again in random order; after that, that order can't tie them to the rest of a visit, to an email address or to a review either.

When you report a review

Which review and the reason you picked. Nothing about you or your browser.

What our providers see

Our database doesn't keep IP addresses. The one brief exception: while you verify, the temporary sign-in (run by Supabase) records the IP address and browser it came from, and those records are deleted along with the sign-in (when your review posts, or within a couple of hours if you don't). Like any website, the companies that run it receive your IP address and browser details when your browser connects to them, and keep their own short-term logs under their own privacy policies: GitHub Pages (hosts the site), Supabase (database and the verification sign-in) and Google Fonts (the site's fonts).

The lease scanner

The scanner reads your lease entirely in your browser. The lease is never uploaded, stored or seen by us or anyone else.

2. On your device

We don't use cookies, and nothing on the site tracks you across other websites. The site keeps a few small items in your browser's storage: your theme and major choices; which reviews you marked helpful or reported (just their ids); short scrambled codes for the leases you reviewed (so the same lease isn't reviewed twice from one browser); whether you've unlocked every review; the monthly visitor ID and visit ID described above; and a copy of the site's files so it opens offline. You can clear all of it in your browser settings at any time.

3. How we use it

To show reviews and the ratings and statistics calculated from them; to keep out fake, duplicate and spam reviews (some reviews wait for a person to check them before they appear); to understand which pages and features people use; to send the occasional emails you signed up for; to respond to messages, reports and legal requests; and to keep the site secure.

4. Sharing

  • We never sell personal information, and we never share email addresses with landlords.
  • Service providers that run the site for us: Supabase (database), GitHub (hosting), Google Fonts, Proton Mail (our email), and, once we start sending updates, an email delivery service. They may use the data only to provide their service.
  • Aggregate statistics that don't identify anyone, like "average rent on Green St" or a landlord's average rating, may be published or included in reports we offer to landlords, property managers or local businesses.
  • Legal requests. If the law requires it (for example a valid subpoena or court order), we may have to disclose information we hold. Because reviews aren't stored with emails, accounts or IP addresses, there is very little that could identify a reviewer. (On a day when very few people verify, the date alone could still hint at which verified review goes with which address to someone with full access to our database, which is one reason that access is limited to the site's admin.)

5. How long we keep it

  • Reviews: while the site runs, unless removed. A removed review is deleted; we keep only a note that it was removed, when and why, never its text.
  • Usage records: deleted after 25 months.
  • Email addresses: until you ask us to delete them. If you unsubscribe, we keep the address marked "unsubscribed" so we never email it again.
  • If you ask us to delete your address, we delete it and keep only a one-way scrambled fingerprint (a hash) of it, so it can never be added back to the list by anyone. The hash can't be turned back into your address.
  • Verification sign-in accounts: deleted when the review posts, or within a couple of hours.
  • Spam protection counts (how many reviews, reports, etc. arrived each hour, with nothing about who sent them): 2 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe with the link in any email we send.
  • Delete your email, or ask what we hold: write to ratemycrib@proton.me.
  • Remove a review you wrote: email us the landlord, the lease dates and roughly when you posted it, so we can find it. Because reviews are anonymous we can't look them up by your name or email, and we may ask a question only the author could answer.
  • Opt out of the visitor ID: turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser.

Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing for advertising. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. Email us to use any of these rights; we'll respond within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

7. Age

RateMyCrib is for college students and other renters. You must be 18 or older to post a review or give us your email. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18; if you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), database rules that keep the public from reading anything but published reviews, and limits on how fast anything can be submitted. Only RateMyCrib's administrators can see email addresses, and the review list and email list are kept separate. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

9. Where your data is

RateMyCrib is run from Illinois, and our providers store data in the United States. If you use the site from elsewhere, your information is processed in the United States.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects how we use email addresses you already gave us, we'll tell you by email before it applies.

11. Contact

Email ratemycrib@proton.me.
