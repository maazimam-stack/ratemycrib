Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a review, we collect: the content of your review, your star ratings, tags you select, and the landlord and property you're reviewing. If you choose to verify your student status, we keep your @illinois.edu email address (see section 3).

To understand how the site is used, we log page views and feature use (for example, searches and lease scans, never the text of a lease) with a random visitor ID that resets every calendar month, a random ID for each visit, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the website that sent you (for example reddit.com). The visitor ID is never attached to a review. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we do not assign a visitor ID.

We also automatically collect basic usage data through our hosting provider, which may include your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

To prevent fraudulent or duplicate reviews, we generate a device fingerprint — a non-identifying hash derived from your browser and device characteristics (such as screen resolution, timezone, and browser plugins). This fingerprint is used solely for anti-fraud purposes. It does not identify you personally and is not shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: display reviews on the platform for other students to read, verify student status when an @illinois.edu email is provided, send occasional updates to students who verified (you can unsubscribe anytime), improve the Service and user experience, and enforce our Terms of Service (e.g., removing fraudulent reviews).

3. Email Verification

If you choose to verify, we send a one-time code to your @illinois.edu address through our database provider, Supabase. When your review posts, it is marked "Verified Illini" and the sign-in account is deleted. We keep your email address, and the day you first verified, in a separate contact list so we can send occasional RateMyCrib updates. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to ratemycrib@proton.me. The contact list holds no reviews, landlords or times; your email is never attached to or displayed with your review, and the site shows only the date a review was posted. We never sell your email or share it with landlords. Supabase keeps routine sign-in logs for a short time for security.

4. Data Storage

Review data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider, with servers located in the United States. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data (e.g., "average rent in Campustown") for research or reporting purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of RateMyCrib and its users.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of a review you submitted by emailing ratemycrib@proton.me with details identifying your review. Since reviews are anonymous, you may need to provide information that confirms you are the original author.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your "helpful" votes so you don't accidentally vote twice, your theme and major choices, and the monthly visitor ID described in section 1. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics that track you across websites.

8. State Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to request disclosure of what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, and opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.

If you are an Illinois resident, we do not collect biometric information. We comply with all applicable provisions of the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act.

To exercise any state privacy rights, email ratemycrib@proton.me with your request. We will respond within 45 days.

9. Data Retention

We retain review data for as long as the Service is operational. If you request deletion of your review, we will remove it within 30 days of verifying your identity as the original author. Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for college students (ages 18+). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, contact us immediately and we will delete the information.

11. International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

12. Do Not Track

The Service does not use tracking cookies and does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because we do not track users across other websites in the first place.

13. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including Row Level Security policies on our database, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and restricted database access. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at ratemycrib@proton.me.
